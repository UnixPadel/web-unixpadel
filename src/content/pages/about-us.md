## Turning Padel Court Dreams into Reality

Based in the historical city of Istanbul, **Unixpadel** is dedicated to crafting exceptional padel courts that redefine your playing experience. We are at the forefront of delivering top-quality standard, panoramic, full panoramic, and concept designs for padel enthusiasts worldwide.

Equipped with an advanced technological machinery production line, our dynamic team handles every step with innovation—from design and production to delivery and installation. Whether it is a cozy backyard court or a large-scale international project, we bring your vision to life.

Our presence extends far beyond Turkey, with dedicated offices in **Romania, Austria, the United States, India, the Netherlands, and France**. We prioritize collaboration, guaranteeing tailored courts that combine longevity, aesthetics, and safety.

- **ITU Tested:** Subjected to rigorous wind resistance and man impact tests by the prestigious Istanbul Technical University.

## Our Vision

To be the global engineering authority that shapes the future of padel through technical excellence and responsible innovation.

At Unixpadel, we see the court not just as a product, but as a complex structure that requires precision engineering. Our vision is to elevate the sport by merging advanced construction technologies with superior gameplay dynamics. We aim to transform padel from a recreational activity into a universally accessible, high-standard experience, regardless of geography or climate.

### Our Strategic Pillars

- **1\. Redefining Global Standards:** We strive to establish “Unixpadel Quality” as the benchmark for the entire industry. By applying rigorous civil engineering norms to every project—from static calculations to material durability—we ensure that our courts stand the test of time. Our goal is to lead the market not just in volume, but in safety, structural integrity, and certification compliance across 94+ countries.
- **2\. Sustainable Innovation & Limitless Technology:** Our vision is to make padel accessible everywhere while protecting the planet we play on. We break physical boundaries with proprietary technologies like the **Floating Court**, **Retractable Roof Systems**, and **Mobile Fold X-Tend**, bringing the game to the world’s most challenging environments. Crucially, we achieve this through eco-friendly engineering—utilizing energy-efficient processes and recyclable materials to ensure that our cutting-edge solutions leave a positive, lasting legacy for future generations.

## Our Mission

- **1\. Deliver Superior Quality:** Produce every padel court with world-class engineering, international certifications and strict quality standards, using **S235/S355 steel**, **ISO 1461 galvanization**, **FIP-approved turf** and **EN12150 tempered glass**.
- **2\. Innovate Continuously:** Develop industry-leading solutions such as **Flat Floor Court**, **Mobile Fold X-Tend**, **Retractable Roof Systems**, **Floating Court** and **Portable Plate Systems**, expanding R&D investments to create modular and climate-resistant structures.
- **3\. Expand Global Accessibility:** Serve more than 94 countries with fully customizable systems designed for local wind loads, snow loads and engineering norms, making padel accessible everywhere.
- **4\. Provide Transparent and Reliable Service:** Offer rapid production, accurate technical guidance and professional project support while treating every client as a long-term partner.
- **5\. Commit to Sustainability:** Implement environmentally responsible manufacturing with energy efficiency, waste reduction and recyclable materials.
