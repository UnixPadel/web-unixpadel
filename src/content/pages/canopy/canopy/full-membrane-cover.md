## Against snow load and wind load

### Introduction and System Overview

#### The Unixpadel Fully Enclosed Padel Court Roof Cover is an engineered steel roof structure specifically designed to withstand heavy snow and wind loads. By integrating the primary and secondary structural framing, the system fully complies with Italian NTC 2018 and Eurocode standards.

#### Flame Resistance and Membrane Specifications

- ##### **Cover Material:** Heavy-duty 900 _g/m²_ PVC membrane with a PVDF topcoat
- ##### **Fire Classification:** EN 13501-1 B-s2,d0
- ##### **Tensile Strength:** ≥ 4000 N/5 cm
- ##### **Tear Strength:** ≥ 600 N

#### Load Capacity and Environmental Adaptation

- ##### **Snow Load:** 1.50 _kN/m²_
- ##### **Wind Effects:** Specific regional wind effects per Vicenza maps
- ##### **Temperature Range:** -20 _°C_ to +60 _°C_
- ##### **Load Combinations:** ULS/SLS under EN 1990

#### Efficiency and Structural Layout

##### The system features optimized frame spacing of 3.0 to 5.0 meters based on static analysis. It incorporates an integrated ridge and gutter drainage system designed to strictly prevent water ponding, alongside built-in snow retention controls and maintenance access points.

## Durability and Corrosion Resistance

- ##### **Structural Framework:** Manufactured from high-quality S355 structural steel, ensuring maximum load-bearing capacity and structural integrity; this enables it to withstand severe environmental stresses and powerful wind forces.
- ##### **Corrosion Protection and Galvanization:** Features a hot-dip galvanized coating with a minimum thickness of 85 μm (fully compliant with the **EN ISO 1461** standard), providing long-term rust prevention and durability for padel club facilities in high-humidity or coastal areas.
- ##### **Heavy-Duty Fasteners and Hardware:** Secured with high-strength **EN 14399** preloaded non-slip bolts and **EN 15048** standard bolts to guarantee absolute joint stability and safety under continuous dynamic loads.
- ##### **Superior Material Quality:** By utilizing high-quality **AISI 304/316 stainless steel** hardware where required, critical connection points are protected against oxidation, significantly extending the service life of the indoor padel canopy.

> **Assembly, Anchoring, and Certification** protocols ensure that the Unixpadel structure offers maximum structural safety and long-term durability. The system anchoring relies on **ETA-approved chemical and mechanical hardware** designed specifically to withstand combined shear and tension forces under extreme weather. To guarantee the highest European quality standards, fabrication is strictly **EN 1090-1 CE certified (Execution Class EXC2)**, providing independent verification of our structural steel reliability. Additionally, the premium membrane installation requires a fully documented, professional tensioning sequence. This rigorous process prevents sagging over time, extends the fabric’s lifespan, and ensures flawless performance for indoor padel court operations.

> **Weather Resistance and Warranty** standards guarantee that the padel court remains operational in all climates. The premium cover membrane is fully **UV, fungus, and weather-resistant**, ensuring long-term color stability and structural integrity against harsh sunlight and moisture. Advanced manufacturing technology ensures that the **welded seams maintain ≥ 90% of the base fabric's strength**, eliminating weak points across the roof canopy. For ultimate safety compliance, all static structural calculations are rigorously validated and certified by a **licensed Italian engineer** to meet strict European building codes. Reflecting this premium engineering quality, the entire system is backed by a comprehensive **10-year warranty** covering both **structural steel corrosion** and **membrane stability**, offering commercial facility owners a secure, long-term investment.
