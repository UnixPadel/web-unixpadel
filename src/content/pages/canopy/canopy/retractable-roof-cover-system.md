## Engineered all-weather roof solution for single and multi-court padel facilities.

#### Introducing Unixpadel Retractable Roof Cover System: Unleash Your Padel Court's Potential

Unixpadel is proud to present our innovative Retractable Roof Cover System, designed to elevate your padel court experience. With advanced features and cutting-edge technology, this system provides versatility, protection, and convenience. Let's explore the exceptional technical details:

#### Customization Options:

**Color Selection:** Choose from a range of colors including Blue, Black, Green, Terracotta, White, or Silver, allowing you to personalize your retractable roof cover system and create a visually appealing court environment.

#### Convenient Retraction:

**Automatic Retraction:** Our system features a special rail system that enables automatic opening and closing of the roof. Enjoy the convenience of retracting the roof effortlessly, transforming your padel court from indoor to outdoor and vice versa.

#### Impressive Finish:

**Protective Coating:** The finish of the system is achieved through powder electric galvanized paint, offering superior protection against corrosion. Optionally, hot-dip galvanization is available for enhanced durability, catering to your specific requirements.

#### Easy Assembly and Disassembly:

**User-Friendly System:** Our retractable roof cover system is designed with ease of assembly and disassembly in mind. Experience hassle-free installation, allowing for convenient maintenance or repositioning of the cover as needed.

#### Effortless Operation:

**On-Off Operation:** Our retractable roof cover system is equipped with automatically controlled motors, allowing seamless on-off operation with ease. Control the opening and closing of the roof effortlessly, adapting to changing weather conditions and your preferences.

#### Superior Membrane:

**High-Quality Membrane:** The 650-gram double-layer membrane is designed to provide optimal performance and longevity. It boasts flame impermeability, ensuring enhanced safety and protection for your padel court.

#### Durable Steel Construction:

**Engineered Steel Structure:**  
The system is built with S235JR or S355 structural steel, with all profiles and sections defined according to project-specific structural calculations. Fabrication is carried out in accordance with EN 1090-1, welding quality follows ISO 3834, and corrosion protection is provided through ISO 1461 hot-dip galvanizing with optional electrostatic powder coating.

#### Exceptional Membrane Features:

**Unrobbed Flame Impermeability:** The membrane's flame impermeability ensures an added layer of safety and protection for your padel court.

**Anti-Fungal and High UV Resistance:** Our membrane is designed to resist fungal growth and withstand prolonged exposure to UV rays, ensuring its longevity and preserving its performance.

**Fastness and Anti-Fracture:** The membrane's fastness and anti-fracture properties prevent bending and cracking, guaranteeing a reliable and durable cover for your padel court.

**Waterproof Capabilities:** With its waterproof design, the membrane effectively shields your court from rain, ensuring that gameplay can continue uninterrupted.

#### Unmatched Functionality:

**Electric Push-Pull Structure:** The electric push-pull structure is a key feature of our padel court tent. It provides efficient heat insulation and protection against the sun and rain, enhancing the comfort of your playing environment.

**Flexibility and Adjustable Ceiling Height:** The retractable roof system allows for flexible internal space, enabling the ceiling height to be freely raised and lowered. Adapt the court space according to your needs and preferences.

#### Elevate Your Padel Court Experience:

Unixpadel's Retractable Roof Cover System is engineered to enhance your playing environment, offering unparalleled functionality, protection, and convenience. Trust in our expertise to create a court that adapts to your needs, ensuring optimal performance and enjoyment.
