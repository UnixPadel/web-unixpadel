#### 2. Steel Fabrication & Surface Protection

##### 2.1 Material Standards

- Minimum steel grade: S235JR or S355
- Fabrication according to EN 1090-1 and EN 1090-2

##### 2.2 Welding

- Welding in compliance with ISO 3834
- Full penetration continuous welding at critical joints

##### 2.3 Galvanization

- Complete hot-dip galvanization
- Coating thickness: 80–100 microns

##### 2.4 Painting (Optional)

- Electrostatic powder coating
- UV-resistant, minimum 10-year durability

#### 4. Customer Responsibilities (Paragraph)

The customer is responsible for foundation works, static verification, and obtaining all licenses and permits. The installation area must be flat, level, obstacle-free, and accessible for heavy machinery. The customer must provide 220V/380V electricity during installation and ensure the safety and protection of all materials on-site. Any delays or issues due to unsuitable foundations, slopes, or missing preparations may result in additional costs. Waste disposal, site cleaning, and material transport within the site are also the customer's responsibility.
