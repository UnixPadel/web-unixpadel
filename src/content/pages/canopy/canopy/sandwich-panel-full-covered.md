A fully enclosed, steel-framed padel court system engineered with insulated sandwich panels  delivering year-round comfort, superior thermal performance, and a durable, weather-resistant structure.

## System Overview

This specification defines the design, engineering, fabrication, supply, installation, and quality control of a steel roof and enclosure system for padel courts located in Germany, executed by Unixpadel.

### The System Includes

- Primary and secondary steel structures (H-profiles)
- Hot-dip galvanized steel components
- Thermal insulated sandwich panel roof and façade
- Glazed façade elements for natural daylight
- Anchorage and foundation connection systems
- Drainage and condensation control elements
- Wind, snow and seismic resistant detailing
- Maintenance and inspection access provisions

## Engineered for All Conditions

Every Unixpadel structure is calculated against the strictest European load conditions, ensuring safety and longevity across diverse climates.

- **Characteristic snow load:** ≥ 1.50 kN/m² (150 kg/m²) on horizontal projection
- **Wind loads:** According to NTC 2018 and Eurocode EN 1991-1-4, based on Germany wind zone
- **Seismic actions:** Evaluated per EN 1998 / NTC 2018, where required by local authority
- **Temperature range:** −20 °C to +60 °C
- **Execution class:** Minimum EXC2 (EN 1090-2)
- **Deflection limits:** L/250 general; stricter for roof panels to prevent ponding

## Premium Steel & Sandwich Panels

### Steel Structure

- **Steel grade:** S355 (EN 10025)
- **Profiles:** H-profiles (columns and main beams)
- **Surface protection:** Hot-dip galvanizing ≥ 85 μm (EN ISO 1461)

### Bolting & Welding

- Preloaded bolts EN 14399 / standard joints EN 15048
- Stainless steel AISI 304 / 316 where exposed
- Welding to EN ISO 3834-2; welders certified EN ISO 9606-1

## Roof & Façade Performance

### Panel Characteristics

- **Core material:** PIR or PUR insulation
- **Thickness:** 60–100 mm (project specific)
- **Thermal conductivity (λ):** ≤ 0.022 W/mK
- **U-value, roof:** ≤ 0.30 W/m²K
- **U-value, wall:** ≤ 0.35 W/m²K

### Fire, Acoustic & Durability

- **Fire reaction:** EN 13501-1 — B-s2,d0
- **Sound insulation:** Rw ≥ 25–30 dB
- **Air & water tightness:** Tested per EN standards
- **Durability:** UV, moisture & corrosion resistant
- **Standard:** EN 14509 factory-made panels

### Certification

- CE marking (EN 14509)
- Thermal performance test reports
- Fire classification certificates
- Acoustic and condensation resistance documentation

### Structural System

- H-profile main frames with 3.0–5.0 m spacing
- Secondary steel members support sandwich panels
- Glazed façade zones for natural lighting
- Integrated gutter and drainage system
- Snow sliding & accumulation control
- Maintenance access lines and anchor points

## Full European Compliance

### Eurocodes

- EN 1990 – Basis of structural design
- EN 1991-1-3 / 1991-1-4 – Snow & wind actions
- EN 1993 – Steel structures
- EN 1998 – Seismic design

### Execution & Quality

- EN 1090-1 / EN 1090-2 – CE marking & execution
- EN ISO 3834-2 – Welding quality requirements
- EN ISO 1461 – Hot-dip galvanizing
- EN ISO 12944 – Durability & corrosion protection

All documentation validated and signed by a licensed Italian structural engineer (Albo).

## Anchorage & Foundations

The connection between structure and substrate is engineered to transfer combined tension and shear forces safely into the foundation.

- Anchors designed for combined tension and shear
- ETA-approved chemical or mechanical anchors
- Installation per manufacturer instructions: drilling, cleaning, curing, torque control
- Compliance with EAD 330232-00-0601
- Reinforcement verified by non-destructive methods where required
