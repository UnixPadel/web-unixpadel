Unixpadel has redefined the limits of where padel can be played.  
With our patented Flat Floor Court System, padel is no longer confined to traditional construction sites. Whether it’s a rooftop terrace overlooking the skyline, a school sports area, a luxury resort, or a temporary event installation, the Unixpadel Flat Floor Court delivers world-class playability without the need for concrete foundations or invasive construction work.

#### 1. Zero Concrete, Zero Limits – Install Anywhere

Forget heavy foundations and disruptive building work. Our innovative pedestal-based system sits directly on any **flat, stable surface**, enabling rapid installation in locations that were once considered impossible for padel courts.  
This means **faster project timelines**, **lower site preparation costs**, and **complete location flexibility** — perfect for both permanent and temporary setups.

#### 2. Engineered Pedestal Foundation – Strength You Can Trust

The court’s base structure relies on specially designed **high-density, load-bearing pedestals**, each tested to support **400 kg/m²**, fully compliant with **EN 1991-1-1 European load standards**.  
These pedestals have been impact-tested, deformation-tested, and precision-engineered to ensure **unmatched stability** during high-intensity games, guaranteeing consistent ball bounce and safe player movement.

#### 3. Marine-Grade Laminated Plywood Flooring – Designed for All Seasons

**Our 18 mm marine-grade laminated plywood panels form the heart of the playing surface. This premium flooring material is:**

- **Water-resistant** – preventing swelling, warping, or decay.
- **Impact-resistant** – absorbing shocks to protect player joints.
- **Anti-mold & anti-fungal** – ensuring hygienic play in all climates.

Unlike ceramic or hard concrete flooring, this surface offers a softer, warmer feel underfoot, making it ideal for cooler climates and year-round use.

#### 4. Heavy-Duty Steel Structure – Built to Withstand Nature

The court’s frame is constructed from **80x80x4 mm hot-dip galvanized steel**, treated with an **epoxy anti-rust coating** for maximum durability.  
Tested to endure wind speeds of up to **120 km/h**, the structure provides **long-lasting strength**, even in coastal or high-altitude environments where weather can be extreme.

#### 5. Premium Tempered Glass Panels – Safety Meets Style

The side and rear walls are crafted from **10 mm certified tempered safety glass**, meeting strict **international impact resistance standards**.  
This ensures:

- **Player safety** – glass is designed to crumble into small, blunt pieces if broken.
- **Visual elegance** – providing clear, unobstructed views for spectators.
- **Durability** – resisting scratches, chips, and weather wear over time.

#### 6. Advanced Membrane Roof System – Play All Year Round

Our **optional retractable or fixed membrane roofing system** turns your padel court into an all-weather venue.

- **PVC-coated polyester membrane** blocks 99% of UV rays, reducing heat buildup and glare.
- Operates in extreme temperatures from **\-30°C to +70°C** without material degradation.
- Integrated **rainwater drainage gutters** ensure the court stays dry even during heavy showers.
- Fully tested for **wind and snow loads**, keeping gameplay uninterrupted in any season.

#### 7. Certified Quality – Built to International Standards

Every Unixpadel court is manufactured under strict ISO-certified processes, including:

- **ISO 9001** – Quality Management
- **ISO 14001** – Environmental Management
- **ISO 45001** – Occupational Health & Safety
- **ISO 3438** – Welding Quality Assurance

All steel structures are **CE-marked and TÜV-certified**, with welding performed according to **EN 1090 standards** to guarantee precision and strength.

Our Flat Floor Padel Courts are already installed in a wide variety of venues worldwide:

- Rooftop sports complexes
- Rooftop sports complexes
- Schools and universities
- Sports clubs and municipal parks
- Shopping malls and entertainment centers
- International tournaments and pop-up events

Whether your project demands a **permanent installation** or a **relocatable solution**, Unixpadel delivers the perfect court to match your vision.

This commitment reflects our belief in **quality without compromise**.
