#### 1. FLAT FLOOR PADEL COURT SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Unixpadel presents its patented Flat Floor padel court system, uniquely engineered for locations without traditional concrete infrastructure. This innovative system supports padel court installation on various surfaces with no need for concrete base works.

**Base Technology:**

- Plastic Pedestal System
- Material: High-resistance plastic composite
- Load Capacity: Each pedestal supports 400 kg/m²

**Certifications:**

- Load-bearing certification per EN 1991-1-1 (Eurocode)
- Verified under ASTM E72 & ISO 10303
- Impact & deformation resistance test certified

**Flooring Surface:**

- Marine Laminated Plywood Panels
- Material: Multi-layered marine plywood (birch core)
- Thickness: 18 mm

**Resistance:**

- Water-Resistant: Complies with EN 314-3 Class 3 (boil-proof)
- Impact-Resistant: Up to 15 J per ISO 3349
- Fungus/Mold Proof: Certified under ISO 846

**Advantages Over Ceramic Tiles:**

- Lightweight
- Higher shock absorption
- Safer in cold/humid weather conditions
- Easy to replace or maintain

#### 2. STANDARD PADEL COURT STRUCTURE (UNIXPADEL MODEL “STANDARD”)

**A. Steel Structure**

- Frame: Hot-dip galvanized steel
- Profile Dimension: 80x80x4 mm
- Coating: Anti-corrosion epoxy powder coating
- Wind Resistance: Designed to withstand wind loads up to 120 km/h
- Fasteners: Stainless steel bolts and anchors

**Durability:**

- Wind Resistance: Up to 120 km/h
- Impact Resistance: Complies with EN 13200-3
- **Steel Material Certification:**
- Grade S235JR / S275JR per EN 10025
- Certified with S (Structural Steel) and J (Toughness Performance) classifications

**B. Glass Panels**

- Material: Tempered safety glass
- Thickness: 10 mm

**Properties:**

- Shatter-resistant per EN 12150
- Impact load tested to 1800 N
- Thermal treatment verification under UNI 7697
- Certified by TÜV Rheinland, SGS, and CE approved

#### 3. UNIXPADEL FLAT FLOOR COVERING SOLUTION

Unixpadel offers a compatible and reliable membrane roofing solution, specifically engineered to meet the unique demands of **flat floor** installations. This system ensures optimal surface protection, durability, and seamless integration with the overall structural design.

**A. Retractable Roof System**

- Type: Motorized telescopic retractable structure
- Operation: Remote-controlled electric drive system with manual override
- Material: Reinforced aluminum and galvanized steel subframe integrated into Unixpadel’s 80x80x4 mm column system
- Opening Mechanism: Cross-track horizontal sliding or accordion-folding panels
- Roof Cover: Double-layer PVC-coated polyester membrane with thermal insulation

**B. Technical Properties**

- Fabric Weight: 950–1100 g/m²
- UV Resistance: >99% UV blocking, certified EN 13758
- Wind Load Resistance: Certified up to 110 km/h (EN 1991-1-4)
- Snow Load Capacity: Up to 120 kg/m² (EN 1991-1-3)
- Temperature Performance: –40°C to +80°C
- Fire Rating: EN 13501-1, Class B-s2, d0
- Acoustic Performance: Optional membrane upgrades available for noise attenuation

**C. Automation & Safety**

- Integrated sensors for wind, rain, and snow detection
- Automated locking and emergency stop mechanisms
- CE-certified electric motors with IP65 protection class
- Ready for integration with smart building management systems
- Designed to accommodate future upgrades such as AI-based weather prediction
- Compatible with various remote control and automation platforms
- Flexible configuration options based on site-specific safety requirements

**D. Structural Certifications**

- Static Load Reports by certified structural engineers
- Eurocode compliance (EN 1991-1, EN 1993, EN 1999)
- U.S. ASCE/SEI-7 and DIN 1055 conformity
- Wind and seismic load analysis tailored to project location and site conditions

**E. Advantages**

- All-weather usability
- Rapid open/close transition (under 60 seconds)
- Enhanced energy efficiency through daylight optimization
- Flexibility for commercial, educational, and sports applications
