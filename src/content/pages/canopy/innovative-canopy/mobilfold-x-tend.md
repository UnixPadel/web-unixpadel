#### Precision Engineering Meets Simplicity

**Specifications at a Glance :**

**\*** Dimensions: 21m x 11m Steel Structure  
**\*** Upper Flange Dimensions: MINIMUM 3MM Thickness  
**\*** Lower Flange  
**\*** Steel Rods: 3MM Thickness  
**\*** Covering Material: PVC Branda (900g/m²), offering exceptional weather resistance and UV protection.

#### Quick, Screw-Tightened Installation

Forget the hassle of traditional, time-consuming installations. This canopy system is designed for fast, screw-tightened assembly, reducing setup time while maintaining uncompromised structural integrity. With no need for a concrete base, this system offers flexibility for various site conditions — even soft ground installations are possible with our new design.

#### Unmatched Stability with 'W' Reinforcement

The standout feature of this canopy system is the 'W'-shaped truss reinforcement integrated into the upper structure. This strategic design enhances the overall stability of the canopy, distributing the load evenly and ensuring the frame holds firm under any environmental conditions. The 21m x 11m steel structure uses minimum 3mm upper flange dimensions and 3mm lower flanges, reinforced by minimum 3mm steel rods to provide superior durability.

#### All-Weather Protection for Your Court

Our PVC Branda covering delivers maximum defense against environmental elements, ensuring year-round protection from rain, wind, and UV rays. Whether under bright sunshine or a sudden downpour, this canopy system keeps your court secure, allowing games to continue without interruption, while ensuring comfort for players and spectators.

#### Designed for Versatility

**\*** No Welding Required: Screw-tightened system for quick assembly and relocation.  
\* Base-Free Construction: Perfect for installations on soft ground, enabling more location flexibility.  
\* Customizable Options: Available in roof-only, side-only, or full-cover configurations.
