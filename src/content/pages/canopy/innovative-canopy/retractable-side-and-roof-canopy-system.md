Unixpadel’s newly developed Retractable Side and Roof Canopy System offers an innovative, flexible, and highly durable solution for padel courts worldwide. Designed to withstand the harshest outdoor conditions and adaptable to all climate zones, this system merges engineering excellence with aesthetic appeal. The following technical data can be directly used in project tenders and specification documents.

#### 1. Structural Design

• Custom-engineered in accordance with EN 1990–1999 Eurocode standards  
  • Adapted to the geographic and climatic conditions of each project site  
  • Wind and snow loads are analyzed based on local building codes and regional requirements  
  • All structural calculations are carried out using certified engineering software (SAP2000, RFEM, Robot Structural Analysis)

#### 2. Steel Quality

• Primary and secondary structural elements use high-strength steel: S355 grade or higher  
  • Conforms to EN 10025-2 standard  
  • All steel materials come with 3.1 certificates for traceability and compliance

#### 6. Load Resistance and Performance Criteria

• Wind Load: Engineered to withstand wind speeds of at least 110 km/h (per EN 1991-1-4)  
  • Snow Load: Designed to resist a minimum of 90 kg/m², or higher depending on geographic location  
  • Static Calculations: Validated by certified engineers; all reports included for tender documentation

#### 7. Operational and Environmental Features

• High UV resistance and heat-reflective technology  
  • Up to 30–40% temperature reduction inside the court  
  • High elasticity and impact resistance prevent thermal deformation  
  • Aerodynamic design minimizes wind effect during play, enhancing gameplay comfort

#### 8. Membrane Specifications

• Material: High-tenacity polyester fabric with PVC coating  
   • Weight: 1100 gr/m² • Features:  
   • UV-blocking and sun-reflective  
   • Fire-retardant: DIN 4102-B1 / EN 13501-1 B-s2,d0  
   • Resistant to extreme heat and cold  
   • REACH and RoHS compliant  
   • Certified under EN 13773 and ISO 3795
