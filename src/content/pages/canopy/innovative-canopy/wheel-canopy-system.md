## Introducing the UnixPadel Wheel System Roof Cover

Effortless Control. All-Weather Protection. Seamless Performance.

#### Versatile Design Meets Reliable Protection

The **UnixPadel Wheel System Roof Cover** is designed to offer unmatched protection for your padel courts, ensuring uninterrupted gameplay in any weather. This innovative system combines **a manual, wheel-operated mechanism** with high-quality materials, delivering a roof that opens and closes with ease.

**Key Specifications**

- **Dimensions:** 21m x 11m for 20m x 10m padel courts
- **Steel Structure:**
      -   Main Support: 4mm thickness (SD35JR steel)
      -   Upper Section: with 3mm thickness
      -   Cross Section: with 5mm thickness
- **Wheel System:**
      -   Wheel Diameter: 3mm
      -   Load Capacity: Up to 3000 kg per wheel
      -   Track System: Corrosion-resistant U-profile tracks (5.25m long)
- **Canopy Material:** PVC Branda (11000 Dtex, 650-800g/m²), providing superior durability, flexibility, and weather resistance.

#### Effortless Manual Operation

The **wheel system** is designed for smooth and efficient manual operation. The **industrial-grade wheels** glide along precision-engineered tracks, allowing one or two individuals to effortlessly retract or deploy the roof. A secure locking mechanism ensures the roof stays in place once positioned, providing peace of mind in any weather.

#### Innovative Sliding Mechanism

Each section of the canopy slides individually along the U-profile tracks, stacking neatly onto the last section when fully retracted. This design offers maximum flexibility and control, allowing you to quickly adapt to changing weather conditions without compromising on aesthetics or performance.

##### All-Weather Protection

- **Rain Protection:** The water-resistant PVC Branda ensures that no water seeps through, while the sloped design efficiently directs water runoff, preventing damage.
- **UV & Heat Protection:** The roof material offers exceptional UV protection, reducing heat buildup and keeping the court cool even during hot weather.
- **Wind Resistance:** The robust frame and secure locking system provide excellent wind resistance, keeping the structure stable and secure during strong winds.

##### Built for Durability

Constructed with S355 JR steel and corrosion-resistant tracks, the UnixPadel Wheel System Roof Cover is built to withstand the toughest environments. The 11000 Dtex PVC Branda is designed for high performance, ensuring long-term durability with minimal upkeep.

**Elevate Your Padel Court Experience**

Maximize your court’s potential with the **UnixPadel Wheel System Roof Cover**. Whether it's blazing heat or a sudden downpour, you can keep your game going. Engineered for ease of use, durability, and all-weather protection, this system is the perfect addition to any professional or recreational padel court.
