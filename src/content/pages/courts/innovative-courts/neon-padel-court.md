The Unixpadel Neon Padel Court is a panoramic padel court with a difference: its frame glows in the dark.

By day it is a clean, bright panoramic court. By night the phosphorescent steel frame and mesh panels light up in neon green, turning the court into the centrepiece of the venue and giving clubs a format no standard court can offer: glow sessions, night events and social media moments.

The structure follows our [Panoramic Padel Court](/panoramic-padel-court/), with pillar-free back walls and 12mm tempered safety glass. It is built with 80x80x2mm steel profiles and reinforced by wind braces at the corners. The court matches standard 20m x 10m FIP dimensions.

## Key Structural Features & Neon Advantages

A panoramic court engineered to stand out after dark.

### Glow-in-the-Dark Frame

- Phosphorescent coating on steel columns, beams and mesh panels.
- Looks like a regular panoramic court in daylight.
- Glows neon green when the hall lights go down.

### 80x80x2mm Profiles & Wind Braces

- Built with 80x80x2mm steel columns.
- Diagonal wind braces stiffen the frame at the glass corners.
- Hot-dip galvanized steel for rust prevention.

### 12mm Tempered Glass

- 12mm EN 12150-1 certified safety glass for maximum impact safety.
- Pillar-free back walls keep the view open for spectators.
- Polished edges and high-strength fasteners.

### FIP Court Dimensions

- Standard 20.00m x 10.00m playing area.
- Dimensions follow International Padel Federation (FIP) guidelines.
- Turf choices in Black, Blue, Green or Red.
