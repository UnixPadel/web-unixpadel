At Unixpadel, we specialize in high-performance padel court solutions that meet and exceed international standards.

The Plate Padel Court is our most robust closed panel model, designed for maximum durability, visual harmony, and environmental resilience. Unlike traditional mesh systems, this premium model uses solid steel plate walls; as a result, it is ideal for high-wind areas, urban installations, and architecturally sensitive projects.

Moreover, we strictly adhere to the standards of the International Padel Federation (FIP), so our courts are always tournament-ready and built to perform.

## Easy installation on existing surfaces

You can install the Plate Padel Court directly on old tennis courts, futsal pitches or football fields — no new concrete foundations required. Consequently, it is an ideal solution for upgrading facilities, roof conversions, or repurposing underutilized sports spaces.

## Performance Highlights

### Wind load resistance

Thanks to its closed panel system, the court suits elevated installations (10+ meters) as well as coastal environments.

### Noise reduction

In addition, the closed steel panels reduce external noise leakage by 30 to 40% compared to mesh fences.

### Low maintenance

Furthermore, the rust-resistant structure requires only routine cleaning and periodic inspections.

## Compliance and standards

- **Dimensions (FIP Standard):** First of all, the standard 20m x 10m court suits both amateur and professional play.
- **Glass safety:** In addition, all glass complies with the EN 12150-1 standard.
- **Engineering:** Furthermore, we provide wind load calculations in accordance with the EN 1991-1-4 standard.

## Customization

- Full RAL color chart available.
- Additionally, we can engrave or print custom logos on the steel panels.

## International Certifications

### Material standards

- CE Marking – EN 1090-1 for steel.

- Welding certificates: EN ISO 9606, EN 1090-2 EXC2, ISO 3438.
- Steel grades: S235JR / S275JR.
- Finally, we ensure full REACH and RoHS compliance.
