## Robust Design: Detailed Static Calculations

Our static calculations for the **Unixpadel portable padel court** ensure safety and reliability by adhering to key Eurocode standard benchmark values: We use Eurocodes 0 through 9, Eurocode 3, including specific guidelines for concrete, steel, and glass, all in a structural design lifetime of 15 years.

Our calculations are performed using advanced calculations made in the SAP2000 program. For fixed loads, the structure features 10mm thick CE-certified tempered safety glass and sturdy steel columns. The glass weight is 25 kN/m³, while wind loads are accounted for with medium-building height elevations and dynamic pressure values. Everyday applications ensure that the structural integrity is robust, reliable, and built to withstand various environmental factors.
