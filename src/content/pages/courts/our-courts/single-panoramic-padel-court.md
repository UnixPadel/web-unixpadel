## Compact Design and Outstanding Performance

The Unixpadel Single Panoramic Padel Court is a refined solution designed for single-player play, combining a compact footprint with an unobstructed panoramic padel experience.

Offering exceptional structural strength against wind loads, this court structure is a safe, durable, and reliable playing environment. Its minimalist panoramic design elevates the visual appeal of the venue while optimizing space efficiency.

To ensure maximum stability and long-term performance, the Single Panoramic Padel Court has undergone comprehensive structural analysis conducted by the technical department of Istanbul Technical University, demonstrating outstanding resistance to wind loads and impact forces, guaranteeing durability and player safety.

## Versatile Indoor & Outdoor Installations

The Unixpadel Single Panoramic Padel Court is suitable for both outdoor and indoor installations, making it a versatile choice for various environments. Whether installed in an open-air setting or within an indoor facility, the court consistently delivers high performance and visual appeal.

With a game area measuring 20 m x 6 m, the Single Panoramic Court is ideal for single padel matches, training sessions, and facilities with limited space. This compact dimension allows players to enjoy high performance without compromising the authentic padel playing experience.

For indoor installations, standard glass options using 10mm or 12mm tempered glass are recommended, combining optimal strength and safety. For outdoor installations, a 100x100x4mm steel structure and 12mm tempered glass provide the necessary durability to withstand harsh weather conditions.
