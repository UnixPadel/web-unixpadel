## Welcome to the UNIXPADEL Standard Padel Court

Welcome to the UNIXPADEL Standard Padel Court, a benchmark in quality and durability. Designed for professionals and enthusiasts alike, our court withstands high loads and provides a smooth, consistent playing experience.

### Unparalleled Structural Stability

We build the Unixpadel Standard Padel Court with structural stability in mind. Heavy-gauge steel profiles form all primary structural members: 100 x 50 x 2 mm columns and 100 x 50 x 3 mm beams. This reinforcement ensures maximum stability during intense play, giving operators and players complete confidence.

### Reinforced Three-Pillar Frame Construction

Unmatched durability and stability defined for tennis and padel environments. Our Standard Court features a reinforced three-pillar frame structure. It provides maximum rigidity, strength, and structural integrity for playing confidence on the court.

## Key Structural Features & Engineering Advantages

Built to withstand environmental forces, heavy wind loads, and intense tournament play.

### 100x50x2mm Steel Profiles

- Heavy-duty 100x50x2mm steel profiles form the main structural pillars.
- Superior resistance against high winds and dynamic player impacts.
- Maximum rigidity ensuring complete player safety during intense rallies.

### Secure Footplate Anchoring

- Heavy-gauge steel footplates anchor directly into concrete foundations.
- High-strength expansion anchor bolts prevent structural movement.
- Absorbs lateral play forces to eliminate frame shifting over long-term use.

### Hot-Dip Galvanized Finish

- A hot-dip galvanized finish and electrostatic powder coating protect all steel profiles.
- 100% weather resistant against UV rays, humidity, and coastal corrosion.
- The court delivers high longevity in both outdoor and indoor installations.

### FIP Regulation Compliant

- The court adheres strictly to International Padel Federation (FIP) 20m x 10m standards.
- EN 12150-1 certified 10mm tempered safety glass panels.
- Electro-welded anti-injury mesh panels for safe and true ball rebound.
