## What is pickleball?

Fast-paced, accessible, and endlessly addictive, pickleball is the fastest-growing sport in the world.

Blending elements of tennis, badminton, and table tennis, it’s played on a compact court with a low net, solid paddles, and a perforated plastic ball. The smaller court size means less running than tennis, making it a hit for all ages and skill levels.

From dedicated sports centers to multi-sport facilities, adding pickleball courts is the fastest way to maximize court utilization, drive venue traffic, and offer a trending racket sport that keeps players coming back.

### WHY ADD PICKLEBALL TO YOUR FACILITY?

- **Fastest-growing sport in the world:** Driving massive global demand for dedicated pickleball courts.
- **Higher player density:** Accommodate more players per square meter compared to traditional tennis courts.
- **Low barrier to entry:** Attracts players of all ages, skill levels, and fitness backgrounds.
- **High ROI potential:** Fast payback period for club owners and sports facilities through high court utilization.

## Official pickleball courts dimensions

Standard pickleball court size is 20 x 44 feet (6.10m x 13.41m) for both singles and doubles play.

The total recommended playing area including safety overrun zones is 30 x 60 feet (9.14m x 18.28m) for tournament play.

Unix Padel manufactures precision-engineered courts following official USA Pickleball and international court standards, guaranteeing official ball bounce, line accuracy, and maximum structural durability.
