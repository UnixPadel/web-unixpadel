## A lighting set designed with the court

Eight MARGAY-300 LED floodlights on four poles light the whole 20 × 10 m playing area of the panoramic court. The layout was simulated on the court itself rather than picked from a table: 470 lx maintained average illuminance with a uniformity (U₀) of 0.74. That meets EN 12193 Class II for club competition and clears Unixpadel's own Premium benchmark of 350 lx.

## Results on the playing area

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Average illuminance (maintained) | 470 lx |
| Minimum / maximum | 349 / 631 lx |
| Uniformity U₀ (minimum / average) | 0.74 |
| Uniformity Ud (minimum / maximum) | 0.55 |
| Maintenance factor | 0.80 |

The result holds when the calculation grid is tightened or the beam changes, with the aiming kept as installed: U₀ 0.72 on a 0.5 m grid, 505 lx with an 80° beam and 440 lx with a 100° beam.

## What the set includes

- 4 poles with 2 MARGAY-300 floodlights each, 8 in total
- Floodlights aimed at 30.2° and 37.8°, without visors
- 300 W and 39,000 lm per floodlight; 6400 K, Ra > 80, 90° beam
- IP65 aluminium housing, full light in under 0.2 s, 2-year warranty

## Power and energy use

The eight floodlights draw 2.4 kW together. At four hours a night, that is about 3,504 kWh a year.

> These figures are calculated with approximate photometry (no LDT file is available) and are maintained values. Out-of-court play is not permitted with this pole layout. Final values must be confirmed by measurement on site.
