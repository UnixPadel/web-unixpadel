#### Indoor Padel Hall with Advanced Thermal Insulation

**Unixpadel** presents the **Full Isolated Padel Court House** — a next-generation indoor sports facility designed to perform flawlessly in **any climate**, from **hot desert conditions (+46°C)** to **extreme cold (-40°C)**.

This fully insulated steel structure offers a comfortable, energy-efficient environment that allows padel enthusiasts to play all year round, regardless of outdoor conditions.

#### Project Overview

The concept features a fully enclosed building housing **6 professional padel courts**. With a **width of 22.5 meters**, **length of 42.5 meters**, and a **clear height of 9 meters**, the space delivers a spacious and modern indoor experience.

The entire facility is insulated using **high-performance PIR sandwich panels**, maintaining an optimal **indoor temperature of 22°C** at all times — even under harsh environmental conditions.

#### Building Envelope & Insulation

Both walls and roof are made of PIR (Polyisocyanurate) sandwich panels that deliver excellent insulation and fire resistance.

Key Performance Features:

- Thermal conductivity: λ = 0.022 W/m·K
- U-value: 0.22–0.25 W/m²K
- Fire classification: B-s2,d0 (EN 13501-1)
- External finish: PVDF-coated steel (RAL 9002/9003)
- Optional skylights for natural daylight

The result: a building that remains cool in the heat, warm in the cold, and highly energy-efficient all year long.

#### Lighting & Electrical

Professional LED sports lighting provides 600–800 lux on the court surface, ensuring excellent visibility without glare.  
All fixtures are IP65-rated and designed for long life and low maintenance.

#### Power systems are divided into separate circuits for HVAC, lighting, and general utilities for efficient energy management.

Fire Safety & Acoustics

The hall is equipped with:

- Fire-resistant doors and panels
- Two or more emergency exits
- Smoke exhaust and ventilation in service areas
- Optional acoustic insulation under the roof for reduced noise

Safety and comfort are prioritized at every stage of design.

#### Structural System

The building’s framework is based on a **galvanized steel portal frame** system engineered for strength and durability.

- Main columns: HEB 320 or tapered welded sections
- Rafters: I-sections with 7–10% roof pitch
- Bolted connections (Grade 8.8) for easy assembly and maintenance
- Full hot-dip galvanization + epoxy paint for corrosion resistance

This ensures long-term stability and reliability, even in coastal or humid climates (C4 corrosion category).

#### Foundations

Each frame is supported by **isolated reinforced concrete pad footings**, built for precision and durability.  
All foundations are made of **C35/45 grade concrete** and reinforced with Ø16 steel bars.  
Anchor bolts are precisely leveled for perfect alignment during the steel erection process.

#### HVAC & Climate Control

The indoor volume of ~9,700 m³ requires an estimated cooling load of 115–135 kW.  
To maintain consistent comfort, the system includes:

- 1 × DOAS air handling unit with energy recovery
- 2–3 recirculating AHUs with textile air ducts
- Air-cooled chiller plant (230 kW total, COP ≈ 3.1)

Indoor Climate Targets:

- Temperature: 22°C
- Relative Humidity: 45–55%
- Air velocity at court level: < 0.25 m/s

This ensures a comfortable and draft-free playing experience.

#### Construction Process

The project follows a clear, efficient construction sequence:

1. -   Site preparation and survey
1. -   Foundation and anchor bolt works
1. -   Steel frame erection
1. -   Wall and roof cladding
1. -   MEP and HVAC installation
1. -   Lighting and commissioning
1. -   Final inspection and handover

Average completion time: 8–10 weeks from site start to delivery.

#### Conclusion

The **Unixpadel Full Isolated Padel Court House** represents the perfect combination of **engineering precision**, **thermal performance**, and **modern sports design**.

Built to withstand any climate and engineered for energy efficiency, it ensures **year-round comfort**, **low operating costs**, and a **premium playing experience**.

Whether located in the **Middle East**, **Europe**, or **North America**, this indoor padel solution delivers **world-class quality — anywhere in the world.**
