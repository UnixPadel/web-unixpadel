Beach clubs, seaside hotels and tropical resorts are some of the best places to run padel courts, and some of the hardest environments for steel and glass. Salt, humidity and wind attack a court every day. The good news: with the right specification, a coastal court lasts as long as an inland one.

## What makes coastal sites hard on padel courts?

- **Salt:** airborne chlorides settle on the structure and accelerate corrosion wherever bare steel is exposed, at scratches, cut edges, welds and fixings.
- **Humidity and condensation:** moisture stays on surfaces for longer, especially inside hollow sections and under fixings.
- **Wind:** open coastlines see strong, gusty winds that load the glass panels and the anchors.
- **Sun and UV:** fade coatings, turf and nets faster than inland.

## How do you protect a padel court by the sea?

Start by classifying the site. ISO 9223 and ISO 12944 rank atmospheres from C1 (very low) to C5 (very high) and CX (extreme, offshore). Most seafront sites are **C4 or C5**, and the coating system should be specified for that category.

On that basis, a coastal court should combine:

- **Hot-dip galvanizing after fabrication (ISO 1461):** every surface, inside and out, including welds and cut edges, coated with zinc.
- **A heavier powder coating system** on top of the zinc, specified for the site's corrosivity category.
- **A4 (316) stainless steel bolts and nuts**, which avoid rust streaks around fixings.
- **EPDM rubber gaskets** between glass and steel, which resist UV and salt.
- **Drainage and ventilation of hollow sections**, so water cannot collect inside the frame.

## What about wind?

Coastal wind is often the larger risk. Ask for a static calculation for your exact location: Unixpadel courts are supplied with static analysis approved by Istanbul Technical University (ITU). On exposed sites, a court with reinforced corners such as the [WP Challenge court](/wp-challenge-padel-court/) and a well-anchored perimeter beam keep the glass stable. If you plan a [canopy](/canopy/), it must be calculated for the same wind.

## How should a coastal court be maintained?

- Rinse the frame, mesh and glass with fresh water every few weeks, and after storms.
- Inspect the coating every three months and touch up any scratch before rust spreads.
- Check fixings and gaskets twice a year.
- Brush and top up the turf sand more often, as wind removes it faster.
