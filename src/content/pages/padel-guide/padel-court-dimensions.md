Understanding official padel court dimensions is essential to build a professional-grade facility or grasp the precise geometry behind the world’s fastest-growing racket sport. Discover the exact measurements, height restrictions, and spatial blueprints authorized for official play.

## Official Padel Court Dimensions Blueprint

![Official padel court dimensions diagram showing the 10m x 20m regulation layout](/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Adsiz-tasarim.jpg)

A regulation padel court is a meticulous architectural setup structured exclusively for doubles play. The official padel court dimensions are a rectangle measuring **10 meters wide by 20 meters long** (internal measurements), split exactly in the middle by a central net, as defined by the [International Padel Federation (FIP) regulations](https://www.padelfip.com/regulations/). This compact blueprint encourages fast positioning, strategic ball placement, and seamless communication between players. The table below summarizes each official padel court dimension at a glance.

## Padel Court Wall Heights and Enclosure Dimensions

The unique dynamic of padel relies entirely on its surrounding structure, so these vertical padel court dimensions matter just as much as the footprint. The enclosure consists of a combination of high-impact transparent tempered glass panels and electronic weld-mesh fencing.

### 1. End Walls (Back Walls)

The back walls must stand at a total height of **4 meters**. The first 3 meters from the ground must consist of a solid rebound surface (typically premium panoramic glass blocks), topped by a final 1 meter of structural metallic mesh fencing.

### 2. Side Walls (Lateral Enclosures)

The International Padel Federation accepts two distinct layouts for side enclosures (stepped or rectangular variant). The standard contemporary design utilizes a combination of 3-meter-high glass blocks extending from the corners, smoothly transitioning into 2-meter-high wire mesh panel dividers toward the center net area.

#### Critical Overhead Clearance Rule

For indoor padel installations, the minimum clear height from the finished turf floor to the lowest structural obstruction (such as roof beams, ceiling trusses, or lighting fixtures) must be **at least 6 meters** across the entire court envelope. For premium international tournaments, a clearance of 8 meters or higher is highly recommended to accommodate high tactical lob shots.

## Line Markings and Surface Dimensions of a Padel Court

All boundary and service lines must be exactly **5 centimeters wide**, painted or integrated in high-contrast white or yellow turf fibers. The surface consists of a professional monofilament textured or fibrillated synthetic turf system infused with specialized kiln-dried silica sand, optimizing grip, predictable ball slide, and uniform rebound metrics. Together with the 10m x 20m footprint, these markings complete the official padel court dimensions required for sanctioned play.
