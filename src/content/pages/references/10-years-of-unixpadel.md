Celebrating **10 Years of Unixpadel**, we look back on a journey driven by a bold vision: to redefine padel court engineering through relentless precision, uncompromising quality, and enduring passion. As a result, ten years of breakthrough innovations and mutual trust have turned that ambition into a recognized global benchmark.

To honor this historic milestone, the entire Unixpadel family gathered for an unforgettable gala dinner on the Bosphorus. Indeed, while sailing between two continents under the illuminated skyline of Istanbul, we celebrated not only the thousands of courts we have built worldwide, but also the extraordinary people who made every achievement possible.

“Behind every court, there is engineering. In addition, within every project, there is deep experience. Above all, behind 10 Years of Unixpadel, there are dedicated people—our people, our team.”

### 10 Years of Unixpadel: Passion & Engineering

For ten years, we have pushed the boundaries of structural performance and padel court architecture. Specifically, every weld, glass panel, and custom profile represents a decade of refined craftsmanship, research, and an unwavering commitment to [International Padel Federation (FIP)](https://www.padelfip.com/) safety and playability standards.

### A Night to Remember on the Bosphorus

Furthermore, milestones are best shared with the ones who make them real. Under the iconic bridges of Istanbul, our designers, factory technicians, project managers, and executive leaders came together to celebrate the collective heartbeat that drives Unixpadel forward every single day.

### Behind Every Project, There Are People

Machinery shapes the steel, but dedicated people breathe life into every project. Therefore, our global reputation is anchored in mutual trust, deep care, and tireless problem-solving that our people bring to every club and federation we serve worldwide.

### One Team. One Spirit. One Vision.

Regardless of the challenge or installation complexity, we move as one cohesive unit. In fact, marking 10 Years of Unixpadel proved that when engineering mastery is backed by genuine teamwork, there are no limits to how far our courts can travel.

## 10 Years of Unixpadel: The Power Behind a Decade

From local dedication to international industry leadership.

### Global Footprint Across 94 Countries

From coastal resorts to indoor clubs and urban rooftops, Unixpadel courts now stand proudly in 94 nations. Consequently, each completed project serves as a testament to our agility in meeting distinct climates, local regulations, and client aspirations.

### Building the Next Decade of Padel

Moreover, our 10th-anniversary celebration is more than a look back—it is the kickoff for our next era of growth. For this reason, we are already engineering new eco-conscious materials, smart relocatable designs, and high-performance solutions for the global padel community.
