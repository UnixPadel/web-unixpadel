We are proud to announce that Unixpadel CEO **Elshan Rasulov** has been officially recognized by the Azerbaijan Business Association in Turkey for his valuable contributions to enhancing trade relations between Turkey and Azerbaijan.

This prestigious recognition reflects a shared commitment to building strong international partnerships, fostering mutual trust, and driving sustainable business growth across borders. It acknowledges not only personal excellence but also Unixpadel's strategic role in connecting markets and communities through world-class padel infrastructure.

At Unixpadel, we see this achievement not only as a personal milestone, but as a testament to our broader vision of creating long-term value through collaboration, innovation, and global expansion. Our mission extends beyond constructing padel courts—we are building bridges between nations, fostering cultural exchange, and establishing the padel sport as a unifying force in the Middle East, Eastern Europe, and beyond.

This recognition further solidifies our commitment to maintaining the highest international standards in every project we undertake. From precision-engineered facilities to professional-grade infrastructure that meets rigorous CE safety regulations and FIP international competition standards, Unixpadel continues to set the benchmark for excellence in the industry.

The Azerbaijan Business Association's honor reinforces our dedication to strengthening economic ties between Turkey and Azerbaijan, two nations whose shared vision for growth and partnership aligns perfectly with Unixpadel's values.

We extend our sincere appreciation to everyone who has been part of this journey—our visionary partners, dedicated team members, and the communities we serve. Together, we continue to grow stronger and transform the global padel landscape. 🚀
