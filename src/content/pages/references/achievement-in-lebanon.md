**Celebrating Our Latest Padel Court Achievement in Lebanon** **🇱🇧**

We’re proud to unveil one of our most exciting projects to date a premium padel court installation in the heart of Lebanon, delivered by the dedicated Unixpadel team. This achievement highlights our passion for quality, innovation, and strengthening the padel community in the region.

**Padel’s Rapid Growth in Lebanon**

Padel is quickly becoming a sensation across Lebanon, attracting people of all ages thanks to its energetic, social, and accessible nature. With growing interest and investment in the sport, our newly installed court marks a major step forward, providing a world-class facility for both casual players and seasoned competitors.

**Built on Trust, Powered by Vision**

This project was made possible through strong collaboration with our Lebanese partners, who shared our vision of creating a standout padel experience. The result? A cutting edge facility that reflects our shared commitment to excellence and community engagement.

**Certified to International Standards**

At Unixpadel, we don’t just build courts we deliver certified performance and lasting value. Our padel courts are:

✅ **CE Certified** — Compliant with EU safety and construction regulations

✅ **FIP Approved** — Ready to host international competitions

✅ **Tournament-Ready** — Designed for top-tier play

**Every Detail Matters**

From surface technology to lighting and fencing, every element of this court was meticulously designed and executed. We’ve created a playing environment that’s not only visually striking but also meets the highest safety and performance benchmarks.

**Seamless Execution, Start to Finish**

This project was completed smoothly, on time, and within budget. Thanks to our dedicated team and trusted partners in Lebanon, we delivered a turnkey solution that reflects our ongoing commitment to excellence in every project.

**Standard Padel Court Dimensions**

📏 **Court Size (FIP Standard)**

- Length: 20 meters
- Width: 10 meters
- Area: 200 m²

🧱 **Walls & Fencing**

- Back walls: 3 meters (glass or solid)
- Side walls: 3 meters (with mesh rising up to 4 meters)

🚪 **Access Points**

- 2 side entries, minimum 1 meter wide

💡 **Lighting**

- 4 to 8 poles, up to 6 meters high, outside the play area

🎾 **Net Specs**

- Height at center: 0.88 meters
- Height at posts: 0.92 meters
- Distance between posts: 10 meters
