**Padel Sport is Rising in Azerbaijan with Unixpadel Quality!**

Interest in **padel** in Azerbaijan is rapidly increasing. One of the driving forces behind this growth is the **Azerbaijani Ministry of Sports** and the **Azerbaijan Tennis Federation,** who have officially endorsed and promoted padel across the country. In this exciting development, **Unixpadel** — a trusted name in over **92 countries** — has been selected as the **official supplier** of padel courts by both the Ministry and the Federation.

**Official and Technically Certified Padel Courts**  
**Unixpadel** courts have been chosen by Azerbaijan’s leading sports authorities for their structural integrity, aesthetic design, and international certifications. Our courts are:

- **CE-certified**
- Built to comply with **EU norms**
- Approved by the **FIP (International Padel Federation)**
- Fully licensed for organizing **international padel tournaments**

**The First International Padel Match in Azerbaijan Played on Unixpadel Courts**

A historic milestone was achieved in Azerbaijan as the country’s **first-ever international padel tournament** was hosted on **Unixpadel’s certified tournament courts**. These courts, approved by FIP, are specifically engineered to meet the demands of global competitions and provide a world-class playing experience.

**Engineering Durability and Advanced Surface Technology**

All structural steel components used in Unixpadel courts are:

- Welded by professionals certified under **ISO 3438**
- Manufactured in compliance with **EN 1090-2** standards
- Made from **hot-dip galvanized** **S235JR or JIS G3101 SS400** **steel**
- Coated according to the **ISO 1461** **galvanization standard**, ensuring at least **15 years of corrosion resistance**

Furthermore, all surfaces are finished with **double-layer electrostatic powder coating**, offering **high resistance to abrasion, scratches, UV rays, and chemicals**. This makes Unixpadel courts highly durable and reliable for both indoor and outdoor use.

**Unixpadel’s Global Power Now in Azerbaijan**

With over **7,800 padel court projects** completed worldwide, Unixpadel is proud to bring its global expertise to Azerbaijan. From Europe to the Middle East, North Africa to Latin America — and now in Baku and beyond — we continue to lead the global padel movement.
