**The Rise of Padel in South Africa**

Padel is taking South Africa by storm, and it’s easy to see why. It’s a dynamic, social sport that’s easy to learn and fun to play, perfect for everyone from kids to seasoned athletes. The South African Padel Association has been a key player in promoting the sport, organizing tournaments, and building a vibrant community of padel enthusiasts. Our new padel court is a big win for this growing scene, offering a top-tier facility for both casual and competitive play.

**Standard Padel Court Measurements**

According to the International Padel Federation (FIP) regulations, a standard padel court measures:

Length: 20 meters

Width: 10 meters

Total Area: 200 square meters

**🧱 Wall and Fence Heights**

Back Walls (Glass or Solid): 3 meters high

Side Walls (Glass and/or Mesh):

Solid wall section: 3 meters high (in the back third)

Mesh fence can reach up to 4 meters total height

**🚪 Access and Doors**

Minimum 2 access points (1 on each side), at least 1 meter wide

**🕯️ Lighting**

Padel courts are typically equipped with 4 to 8 light poles, each up to 6 meters high, positioned outside the playing area.

**🎾 Net Specifications**

Net Height at center: 0.88 meters

Net Height at posts: 0.92 meters

Post distance: 10 meters apart (court width)
