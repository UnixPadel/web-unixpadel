Engineered for Coastal Winds, Built for Pure Performance: 2 New Panoramic Padel Courts Delivered in Çeşme, İzmir!

Padel continues its rapid rise across the globe. Unixpadel proudly announces our latest showcase project in Çeşme, İzmir. We engineered, manufactured, and installed two custom Panoramic Padel Courts. They withstand severe coastal weather while delivering an unparalleled playing experience.

We designed this project for luxury resorts, athletic clubs, and boutique developments. It combines architectural elegance with industrial-grade durability. The new installation transforms the venue into an active hotspot welcoming players year-round.

Bespoke coastal engineering meets international FIP standards for an uncompromising padel experience.

### High-Wind Coastal Engineering

Open-air coastal environments demand rigorous static calculations. Our reinforced steel framework and heavy-duty glass anchors withstand severe coastal gales. This engineering ensures maximum safety and long-term structural integrity.

### Unobstructed Panoramic Views

Our panoramic glass architecture eliminates corner and intermediate pillars. This design creates a seamless, open-court atmosphere for competitors. Spectators and broadcast cameras also enjoy an uninterrupted, 360-degree viewing experience.

### FIP-Certified Tournament Quality

We build every court strictly to International Padel Federation (FIP) regulations. Each component—from tempered safety glass to competition turf—is tournament-ready. The facility easily hosts both casual matches and official championships.

### Marine-Grade Anti-Corrosion Protection

Salty coastal air and high humidity require uncompromising protection. Our dual-layer electrostatic powder coating and anti-corrosive primer shield the metal framework against rust. This finish preserves a pristine showroom look for years to come.
