Another International Milestone: 4 Panoramic Courts for the FIP Tournament!

We are proud to announce the completion of another international milestone! Unixpadel has successfully designed, manufactured, and delivered 4 official FIP-standard Panoramic Padel Courts .for the prestigious [FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/) Tournament in Diyarbakır, Turkiye.

Engineered specifically for professional international competition, these courts combine premium panoramic visibility, high-strength galvanized steel construction, certified tempered glass, and tournament-level playing performance.

Every installation is developed according to strict international engineering standards, ensuring long-term durability, maximum player safety, and outstanding aesthetics.

### Premium Panoramic Visibility

An uninterrupted sporting experience for spectators and players alike. Our courts are designed to provide maximum, unobstructed visibility so everyone can experience every detail of the match.

### Tournament-Level Performance

Designed to meet the rigorous expectations of professionals worldwide. The playing surface, ball bounce, and overall game flow are perfectly aligned with international competitive standards.

Built using high-strength galvanized steel in accordance with top engineering norms, our courts offer superior resistance against harsh weather conditions and heavy, continuous usage.

Thanks to certified tempered glass and advanced impact absorption, players are kept safe even during the fastest and most demanding rallies. Safety and durability are standard for us.
