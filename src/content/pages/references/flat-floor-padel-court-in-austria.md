The Future of Padel Starts Without Concrete.

Why spend weeks on foundations when innovation can be installed in days? Unixpadel’s Flat Floor Padel Court is a next-generation engineering solution designed for existing flat surfaces with no concrete foundation, no drilling, and minimal site preparation.

We engineered this system to eliminate the traditional barriers of padel court construction. By bypassing heavy civil work, we help club operators, developers, and municipalities build smarter, install faster, and get players onto the court sooner.

Designed for maximum performance with minimum site preparation, our Flat Floor Court redefines how quickly your club can grow.

### No Concrete, No Drilling

Forget about pouring concrete or drilling into existing ground. Our innovative system rests securely on your existing flat surface without requiring invasive and permanent groundwork. This means protecting the underlying area while significantly lowering civil construction costs.

### Faster Installation

Time is revenue. By eliminating the need for foundational curing and complex site prep, the Flat Floor Padel Court goes from delivery to play in record time. You can skip the delays of traditional construction and start welcoming players much sooner.

### Modular & Relocatable

Your investment is no longer permanently tied to a single piece of land. The smart modular design allows the entire court to be easily dismantled, transported, and relocated to a new venue if your business needs change in the future.

### Long-Term Performance

Despite its temporary footprint, this court delivers permanent quality. Engineered with high-grade steel and precision manufacturing, it provides the exact same premium playability, ball bounce, and structural integrity as our traditional foundational courts.

## Smart Investment, Better Courts

The definitive choice for venues that require high adaptability and uncompromising quality.

### Lower Civil Costs

By completely removing excavation, concrete pouring, and ground anchoring from your project scope, you dramatically reduce your overall civil construction expenses before the court even arrives.

### Ready for Any Flat Surface

Whether you have an existing warehouse floor, a solid asphalt lot, or a multi-purpose sports hall, as long as the surface is flat and robust, our modular system is ready to be deployed immediately.
