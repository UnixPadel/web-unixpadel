Premium Padel Arrives in Vienna Without Concrete.

We are proud to announce the successful delivery of two Unixpadel Flat Floor Systems featuring our signature Panoramic Padel Courts in Vienna, Austria. Our next-generation engineering brings the ultimate padel experience directly to the city, bypassing the hassle of traditional foundations.

Designed for maximum flexibility, our proprietary Flat Floor System allows a premium padel court to be installed instantly. It is the perfect solution for existing sports facilities, rooftops, temporary venues, rental projects, and locations where civil works are limited.

A professional-grade, relocatable solution that eliminates concrete foundations and speeds up your club’s opening day.

### 2× Panoramic Courts

Our stunning Panoramic Courts offer an unobstructed view, creating an immersive experience for players and spectators alike. Built for premium aesthetics and professional performance in any setting.

### No Concrete Foundation

Skip the heavy civil works. Our Flat Floor System rests securely on existing flat surfaces, completely eliminating the need for concrete pouring, excavation, or ground drilling.

### Modular & Relocatable

Adapt to any business model. The entire court structure is fully modular, allowing you to easily dismantle, transport, and relocate it—ideal for temporary events and rental projects.

### FIP-Approved Quality

Relocatable doesn’t mean compromising on quality. As a FIP-Approved Manufacturer, we ensure our courts deliver the exact same true ball bounce and structural stability as permanent installations.

### Built for Rental Projects

For temporary venues or seasonal rental projects, the ability to install and remove courts rapidly means you can generate revenue faster and adapt your location strategy as needed.
