The International Stage for Sports Facilities

Once again, FSB 2025 in Cologne stood out as the international trade fair for public spaces, sports and leisure facilities. Indeed, it remains one of the most important meeting points in the global sports-infrastructure industry. Moreover, we build and ship courts to more than 90 countries. So this fair is exactly where our customers are: club operators, developers, distributors, and municipalities planning their next facility.

Naturally, Unix Padel exhibited at FSB 2025 with a full-scale court installation. In doing so, we presented the panoramic and standard court systems we manufacture at our own facility. As a result, visitors could step onto the court. There, they inspected the glass, the steel structure, and the canopy in person. Ultimately, that is the difference between a brochure and a supplier you can actually evaluate.

## Elevating Padel in the Heart of Europe

Today, padel is one of the fastest-growing sports on the continent. Central Europe, in particular, is expanding faster than almost anywhere else. Consequently, FSB Cologne brings the decision-makers behind that growth into one hall. There, year after year, Unix Padel meets them.

### Chapter 1: Show, Don’t Tell

A specification sheet can claim anything; a court you can walk onto, however, cannot hide. Therefore, at FSB, buyers examined our tempered glass, our hot-dip galvanized steel structure, and our synthetic turf directly. They judged build quality with their own hands, rather than trusting a PDF. For serious buyers, in short, this is the only evaluation that counts.

### Chapter 2: The DACH Market, Face to Face

First of all, Germany, Austria, and Switzerland are priority markets for Unix Padel. FSB Cologne, naturally, is their hub. Accordingly, we spoke with operators and partners in person at FSB 2025, rather than through email chains across time zones. Those conversations turned interest into real projects. In fact, many clubs now on the ground began as a handshake in Cologne.

![Unix Padel exhibition stand at FSB Cologne with panoramic padel court and branded display wall](/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/fsb-2.png)

### Chapter 3: Engineering, With People Who Care

Typically, facility developers don’t ask about colours. Instead, they ask about foundations, wind loads, drainage, and installation timelines. For that reason, FSB puts our technical team in front of exactly these questions. We engineer our courts to answer them. Specifically, we design them to reduce on-site groundwork and shorten the path from order to first serve.

### Chapter 4: One Manufacturer, Full Control

Notably, every court on display comes from a single manufacturer. We control the entire process, from steel fabrication in our own factory through to final installation on site. As a result, there are no subcontracted quality gaps and no divided responsibility. Ultimately, this is what lets us stand behind the courts we install in more than 90 countries.

## What We Showed at the Stand

In particular, our FSB installation featured the court systems that clubs and developers worldwide request most.

### Panoramic & Standard Padel Courts

To begin with, **panoramic courts** deliver full glass walls with minimal structural interruption. They therefore remain the premium choice for flagship clubs and commercial venues. **Standard courts**, by contrast, are the reliable, high-value configuration for clubs scaling up capacity. In either case, we build both to FIP-standard dimensions and specifications.

### Canopy Systems & Fast-Install Engineering

Furthermore, our **canopy and covering systems** enable year-round play in every climate, from the Gulf to Northern Europe. In addition, our **fast-install engineering** minimises on-site groundwork. Above all, that is a decisive advantage for operators who need courts generating revenue sooner.

Moreover, we deliver them across 90+ countries. So, if you visited us at FSB 2025 in Cologne, thank you. Above all, let’s continue where we left off.
