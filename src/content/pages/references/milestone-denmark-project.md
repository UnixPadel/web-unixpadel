**International Growth: Denmark Project**

Unixpadel is thrilled to announce the successful completion of a landmark project in Denmark, a significant milestone in our ongoing international expansion. This achievement underscores our commitment to delivering unparalleled quality and service, reinforcing our position as a leader in the industry.

**The Rise of Padel in Denmark**

Padel is rapidly gaining popularity in Denmark, and for good reason. This dynamic and social sport is easy to learn, fun to play, and perfect for all ages and skill levels. The Danish Padel Association has been instrumental in promoting the sport, organizing tournaments, and fostering a growing community of padel enthusiasts. Our new padel court in Denmark is a significant addition to this growing scene, providing a top-tier facility for both casual players and competitive athletes.

**Top-Quality Padel Courts, Crafted with Care**

From the very beginning, our goal was to create a padel space that would stand out for its quality and design. Our padel courts are built using the finest materials and the latest technology, ensuring a playing experience that is both enjoyable and safe. Every detail, from the court surface to the lighting and fencing, was meticulously planned and executed to meet the highest standards.

**At Unixpadel, we are committed to certified excellence. Our padel courts are:**

- CE-Certified: Built to comply with EU norms.
- Approved by the FIP (International Padel Federation): Ensuring they meet the highest international standards.
- Fully Licensed for Organizing International Padel Tournaments: Making them a premier choice for competitive play.
- Engineering Durability and Advanced Surface Technology

**UNIXPADEL Certified by FIP & Internationally Recognized**

- Quality and compliance are at the core of everything we build. This padel court proudly carries multiple certifications, including:

- ✅ FIP Approved — recognized by the Federación Internacional de Pádel (FIP)
- ✅ **CE Certified** — built according to stringent EU construction standards
- ✅ **Tournament-Ready** — licensed for hosting international padel competitions

**How Unixpadel Courts Are Suited for Danish Weather:**

- ✅ **Wind-Resistant Engineering**  
      Our courts are designed to withstand wind loads of **130–160 km/h**, making them ideal for Denmark’s stormy days and coastal conditions.
- ✅ **Snow Load Capacity**  
      Unixpadel court covers and structures are engineered to bear **heavy snow loads**, ensuring year-round structural safety.
- ✅ **Galvanized Steel Frame (ISO 1461)**  
      All frames are **hot-dip galvanized**, providing **15+ years of corrosion resistance** — crucial for Denmark’s humid, salty, and rainy environment.
- ✅ **Weatherproof Turf & Glass**  
      We use **UV-resistant, anti-slip turf** and **12mm tempered glass** that performs well in wet, frosty, or fluctuating temperatures.
- ✅ **Optional Covered & Retractable Roofs**  
      Perfect for Denmark’s unpredictable weather — allowing for all-season use without worrying about rain or snow interruptions.

**All structural steel components used in Unixpadel courts are:**

- Welded by Professionals Certified Under ISO 3438
- Manufactured in Compliance with EN 1090-2 Standards
- Made from Hot-Dip Galvanized S235JR or JIS G3101 SS400 Steel
- Coated According to the ISO 1461 Galvanization Standard, ensuring at least 15 years of corrosion resistance.

Furthermore, all surfaces are finished with a double-layer electrostatic powder coating, offering high resistance to abrasion, scratches, UV rays, and chemicals. This makes Unixpadel courts highly durable and reliable for both indoor and outdoor use.
