The choice of steel for a padel court is far more than a matter of construction material — it is a **long-term investment in performance, safety, and aesthetics**. Courts are often subjected to harsh conditions: blazing sun, heavy rain, humidity, and in some cases, the corrosive effects of coastal salt air. Selecting the right steel type ensures that the court will remain **structurally sound, visually appealing, and low-maintenance** for years to come.

### Most Suitable Steel Types

#### 1. Galvanized Steel

Galvanized steel is widely recognized as a **reliable and cost-effective choice** for both indoor and outdoor padel courts.

- **Corrosion Resistance:** Thanks to the hot-dip galvanization process, the steel is coated with a protective layer of zinc, which shields it from rust and environmental wear.
- **Exceptional Durability:** Performs reliably in varying climates, from dry heat to high humidity, without losing its strength or stability.
- **Structural Integrity:** Its mechanical strength ensures the court framework remains stable, even under high-impact play.
- **Best Use Cases:** Ideal for projects where **cost efficiency** is important, yet **longevity** and **safety** cannot be compromised.

#### 2. Stainless Steel (Grades 304 & 316)

For projects that demand superior corrosion resistance and premium aesthetics, stainless steel stands as the ultimate solution.

- Grade 304: Perfect for indoor installations and moderate outdoor conditions. It offers a polished, modern appearance that resists staining and surface damage.
- Grade 316: Specifically engineered for coastal and marine environments, where airborne salt can accelerate corrosion in other metals. This grade ensures maximum protection and lifespan, even in the harshest conditions.
- Long-Term Value: Minimal maintenance is required, and the steel retains its visual appeal for decades — making it the material of choice for high-end, showcase courts.

#### Essential Certifications and Standards

Compliance with international standards is not optional — it is the guarantee that your steel structure meets the highest expectations for safety, performance, and durability.

- ISO 1461 – Galvanization Standard  
      Specifies the quality requirements for hot-dip galvanized coatings, ensuring prolonged corrosion protection.

- EN 10025 – Structural Steel Standard  
      Defines the mechanical and structural properties needed for strength and stability.
- ISO 9001 – Quality Management System  
      Confirms that the manufacturing process is consistent, reliable, and meets customer expectations.
- EN 1090 – CE Marking for Steel Structures  
      A mandatory European standard ensuring that structural steel components comply with EU regulations.
- ISO 9223 – Atmospheric Corrosion Standard  
      Categorizes environmental corrosivity, guiding engineers in selecting the optimal steel grade for the installation site.

#### Final Recommendation

When it comes to building a padel court, the **right steel choice** ensures not only a solid playing experience today but also a **structure that stands the test of time**.

- For **most standard applications**, **galvanized steel** delivers outstanding value, combining affordability with excellent protection.
- For **prestige projects** or locations exposed to **high humidity or salty air**, **stainless steel 316** offers unmatched performance and a flawless finish.
- Adhering to the **correct certifications and standards** guarantees that your court meets international benchmarks for safety and quality.

A well-selected steel type is the foundation of a court that remains **strong, safe, and visually impressive — season after season, year after year**.
