The **Padel center** features a state-of-the-art **full membrane canopy system** designed and installed by Unixpadel to provide year-round protection for padel courts in Northern Italy. Engineered with a high-performance steel structure and weather-resistant membrane, this cover ensures optimal playing conditions in all weather, while enhancing the visual and architectural appeal of the facility.

The membrane is made from flame-retardant, UV-resistant fabric that offers excellent tensile strength and durability, while the underlying steel frame—constructed from S355-grade profiles and hot-dip galvanized—is compliant with European structural standards. With integrated thermal comfort, structural flexibility, and minimal maintenance requirements, this installation reflects the perfect balance between form and function.

The Padel center cover stands as a showcase of Unixpadel’s expertise in delivering **premium padel infrastructure**, combining innovative engineering, sustainable materials, and elegant design to elevate the playing and spectator experience.
