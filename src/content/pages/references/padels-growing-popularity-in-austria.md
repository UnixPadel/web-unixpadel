**Our Latest Padel Court Project in Austria: A Big Win!**

We’re super stoked to share one of our most recent and awesome projects—a brand-new padel court in Austria, thanks to the hard work of the Unixpadel team. This project really shows how much we care about quality, innovation, and building a strong community.

**Padel’s Growing Popularity in Austria**

Padel is really taking off in Austria, and it’s no wonder why. It’s a dynamic, social sport that’s easy to learn and a blast to play, perfect for everyone from kids to seasoned athletes. The Austrian Padel Association has been a key player in promoting the sport, organizing tournaments, and building a vibrant community of padel enthusiasts. Our new padel court in Austria is a big win for this growing scene, offering a top-tier facility for both casual and competitive play.

**Partnership Built on Trust and Vision**

This project was a true team effort. Our Austrian partners trusted us to deliver, and we were all on the same page about creating a fantastic padel space for players of all levels. The collaboration was seamless, and we’re super excited about the final result—a facility that’s sure to be a hit with the local community.

**Certified Excellence in Every Aspect**

At Unixpadel, we don’t just build courts; we build certified excellence. Our padel courts are:

- **CE-Certified**: Built to comply with EU norms.
- **Approved by the FIP (International Padel Federation)**: Meeting the highest international standards.
- **Fully Licensed for Organizing International Padel Tournaments**: Perfect for both casual play and serious competition.

**UNIXPADEL Certified by** FIP  **& Internationally Recognized**

- Quality and compliance are at the core of everything we build. This padel court proudly carries multiple certifications, including:

- ✅ FIP Approved — recognized by the Federación Internacional de Pádel (FIP)
- ✅ CE Certified — built according to stringent EU construction standards

**Durability and Advanced Surface Technology**

We take pride in the durability and advanced technology of our padel courts. All the structural steel components are:

- **Welded by Professionals Certified Under ISO 3438**
- **Manufactured in Compliance with EN 1090-2 Standards**
- **Made from Hot-Dip Galvanized S235JR or JIS G3101 SS400 Steel**
- **Coated According to the ISO 1461 Galvanization Standard**, ensuring at least 15 years of corrosion resistance

**How Unixpadel Courts Are Suited for Austrian Weather:**

- ✅ **High Snow Load Resistance**  
      Unixpadel courts are built to handle **heavy snow loads** — essential for Austria’s alpine and central regions in winter.
- ✅ **Wind Resistance up to 160 km/h**  
      Our steel structures are engineered to resist strong wind loads — suitable for both lowlands and mountainous areas.
- ✅ **Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel Frame (ISO 1461)**  
      All metal components are **corrosion-resistant**, with **15+ years of protection** against Austria’s frost, rain, and humidity.
- ✅ **UV-Resistant, Anti-Slip Turf**  
      Designed for **summer heat and winter resilience**, the turf maintains performance and safety in all seasons.
- ✅ **12mm Tempered Glass**  
      Shatter-resistant and safe for fluctuating temperatures, common in Austria’s spring and autumn.
- ✅ **Covered & Retractable Roof Options**  
      Ideal for all-weather use — protection from **snow, rain, or direct sun**, extending the playing season year-round.
