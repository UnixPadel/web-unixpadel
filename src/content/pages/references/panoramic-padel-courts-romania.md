Another Milestone: Panoramic Padel Courts Delivered to Romania!

Four Unixpadel **Panoramic Padel Courts** have successfully arrived in Romania and are ready for installation. Every delivery reflects our commitment to engineering excellence, premium manufacturing, and long-term structural performance for professional padel venues.

From design to production, every Unixpadel court is manufactured to meet international quality standards and built for professional play.

### Premium Steel Engineering

Engineered to perform. Built with a robust hot-dip galvanized structure, our **Panoramic Padel Courts** are designed to withstand the toughest conditions while maintaining peak structural integrity for years to come.

### Flawless Powder-Coated Finish

Every court features a high-quality powder-coated finish, ensuring a sleek, premium look that resists wear, tear, and weather elements in any environment.

### FIP Standard Panoramic Padel Courts

Manufactured strictly according to [FIP (International Padel Federation)](https://www.padelfip.com/) standards, guaranteeing an official professional playing experience for athletes at all levels.

### Unobstructed Panoramic Views

Our Panoramic models offer an unobstructed, spectacular view of the game, creating an immersive experience for both players on the court and spectators watching the match.
