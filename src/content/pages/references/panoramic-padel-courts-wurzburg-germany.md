Premium Engineering Delivered to Germany: 2 Panoramic Courts Live in Würzburg!

Unixpadel continues its international expansion across Europe with another landmark project delivery! We are proud to announce the successful installation of two high-performance **Panoramic Padel Courts** in Würzburg, Germany.

Combining a modern Anthracite Grey steel framework with striking FIP-approved black artificial turf, this state-of-the-art facility delivers superior playability, modern aesthetic appeal, and long-term structural durability.

Engineered for performance and built for the future — trusted by sports clubs, investors, and municipalities in more than 94 countries worldwide.

### FIP-Approved Black Turf

Featuring certified high-density black artificial grass that meets strict International Padel Federation standards. Provides optimal ball bounce, excellent foot traction, and a distinct luxury visual signature.

### Anthracite Grey Steel Structure

Crafted with precision structural steel finished in a contemporary Anthracite Grey powder coating. Designed to blend seamlessly with modern European architectural standards while offering maximum frame rigidity.

### Advanced Anti-Corrosion Defense

Engineered specifically for variable Central European weather conditions. Multi-layer anti-corrosion coatings and rust protection ensure long-term durability and low-maintenance operation season after season.

### Unobstructed Panoramic Vision

Our column-free panoramic glass design eliminates visual barriers, providing spectators with clear 360-degree viewing and giving players an open, arena-quality atmosphere on the court.

## German Quality Standards Meets Unixpadel Innovation

Elevating padel infrastructure for clubs, sports centers, and private investors across Germany and Europe.

### Turnkey European Logistics

From high-precision manufacturing to direct logistics and expert on-site assembly, Unixpadel provides a hassle-free, complete turnkey service for project developers across Europe.

### High Commercial ROI

Designed for heavy commercial utilization with minimal downtime. Unixpadel courts deliver high player attraction, fast booking turnover, and long-term financial yield for sports facility operators.
