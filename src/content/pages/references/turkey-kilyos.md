## Kilyos Padel Court Installation Project - A New Standard in Excellence

We are thrilled to showcase our new project in Kilyos, an environment comprising of three modern padel courts that will take the game’s enjoyment and participation to a more advanced level for all players irrespective of the level of play.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2.jpg)

### Project Overview

Our team worked hand in hand with local stakeholders to pursue this dream and turn Kilyos into simo one of the best padel hub in the region. The courts are constructed from modern composites and technologies to give extended life, good ball rebound and player protection.

### Key Features

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/3-1.jpg)

• **Professional-Grade Courts:** Each court meets the highest standards for competitive play, with precise dimensions and superior surface quality.

• **Durable Construction:** Engineered for all weather conditions, these courts are designed to withstand heavy use while maintaining optimal performance.

• **Vibrant Color Scheme:** The courts feature a stunning blend of green, padel blue, and red, adding a dynamic and eye-catching aesthetic to the facility. The green provides a natural feel, while the blue enhances focus during gameplay, and the red accents offer a bold contrast, making the courts visually striking and modern.

• **Advanced Lighting System:** Our LED lighting ensures perfect visibility for players, day or night, enhancing the overall padel experience.

• **Sustainability:** As part of our commitment to the environment, we utilized eco-friendly materials and energy efficient systems throughout the project.

## Enhancing Kilyos' Natural Beauty

Kilyos is known for its breathtaking scenery and vibrant outdoor lifestyle, making it the ideal location for these cutting-edge padel courts. With their elegant design and striking color combinations, UnixPadel courts perfectly match the aesthetic of the surrounding environment. These courts not only enhance the area’s visual appeal but also create a new hub for sports enthusiasts, attracting both locals and visitors eager to enjoy the game in a stunning setting

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/4.jpg)
