Unixpadel takes pride in offering high-quality artificial turf solutions for padel courts. Our ADLX4T Artificial Turf system is designed to enhance the performance and durability of your padel court surface, both for indoor and outdoor areas.

#### Superior Filament Technology:

Our ADLX4T Artificial Turf system features Unixpadel turf prefibrillated polypropylene filaments. These filaments are specifically engineered to improve characteristics related to friction, resilience, and resistance to abrasive wear. The advanced technology used in our turf filaments ensures enhanced durability and resistance, making them ideal for the demanding nature of padel gameplay.

#### Approved by International Padel Federation (FIP):

At Unixpadel, we prioritize the quality and compliance of our products. Our Artificial Turf is FIP approved, meeting the strict standards set by the International Padel Federation. To ensure the highest level of quality and performance, our turf has been rigorously tested and certified by KIWA laboratory in the Netherlands. With Unixpadel's FIP-approved Artificial Turf, you can trust that you are playing on a surface that meets international standards.

#### Designed for Optimal Performance:

The ADLX4T Artificial Turf system is meticulously designed to provide exceptional performance on padel courts. With a yarn height ranging from 12mm to 13mm, our turf offers the perfect balance between comfort and playability. The curled PP filament yarn provides excellent resilience and responsiveness, allowing players to make precise shots and movements with ease. Additionally, the infill of sand further enhances the stability and performance of the turf.

#### Versatility for Indoor and Outdoor Use:

Whether you have an indoor padel court or an outdoor facility, our ADLX4T Artificial Turf system is suitable for both environments. Its robust construction and resistance to varying weather conditions make it a reliable choice for any setting. Experience consistent playability and durability, regardless of the location of your padel court.

In addition to our padel court installations, we also offer the option to purchase our high-quality Artificial Turf separately. If you are looking to upgrade or replace the turf on your existing padel court, Unixpadel provides a range of options to suit your specific needs. Transform your court with Unixpadel's trusted and reliable Artificial Turf.
