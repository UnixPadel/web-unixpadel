Unixpadel takes great pride in offering top-quality artificial turf solutions for padel courts, including our renowned Cleek turf. Our Cleek artificial turf is specifically designed to meet the unique requirements of padel court surfaces, providing exceptional performance and durability.

#### Designed for Padel Courts:

Cleek artificial turf is meticulously engineered by Unixpadel to enhance the playing experience on padel courts. Our turf system consists of prefibrillated polypropylene filaments that offer numerous benefits. The Cleek turf filament is designed to improve friction, resilience, and resistance to abrasive wear, ensuring a high-quality playing surface that can withstand the rigors of intense gameplay.

#### Durability and Resilience:

We understand the importance of durability and long-lasting performance in padel court surfaces. The Cleek artificial turf is specifically engineered to deliver exceptional durability and resilience. The characteristics of the filament, combined with our expert design and manufacturing processes, ensure that Cleek turf maintains its quality and performance over time.

#### FIP Approved:

At Unixpadel, we prioritize delivering products that meet the highest standards in the industry. All our artificial turfs, including Cleek, are FIP approved by the International Padel Federation. This certification guarantees that our turfs meet the stringent requirements and quality standards set by the federation. To ensure compliance, Cleek turf undergoes rigorous testing and certification by KIWA laboratory in the Netherlands.
